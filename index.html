Our Story

Chess education,
reimagined

Chess Masters was founded on a single belief: that structured, personalized coaching guided by FIDE-rated professionals can transform any player at any age.

🎯 Our Vision

To become India's most trusted chess development platform — where data-driven coaching meets the timeless art of chess thinking, creating the next generation of Indian grandmasters.

🚀 Our Mission

Making elite chess coaching accessible to every Indian student regardless of geography, through a blend of technology, certified instruction, and structured competition pathways.

Founder's Story

Why Chess Masters was built

GM Vikram Iyer, a 2550-rated grandmaster from Chennai, noticed a critical gap: India was producing chess talent, but the coaching infrastructure was fragmented and unstructured. Top coaches were inaccessible. Parents couldn't track progress. Students had no clear roadmap.

In 2021, he founded Chess Masters with co-founder Ananya Krishnan (ex-IIT, chess software engineer) to build what he wished had existed — a platform combining grandmaster-level instruction with technology that tracks every improvement and connects students with exactly the right coach.

Teaching Philosophy

How we teach chess

Our methodology blends classical chess pedagogy with modern AI-assisted analysis to deliver results faster than traditional coaching.

01

Assess First

Every student begins with a comprehensive position assessment — puzzles, game analysis, and interview — to benchmark strengths, weaknesses, and learning style.

02

Pattern Recognition

Chess improvement is pattern recognition. Our structured puzzle library builds tactical vision methodically — mating patterns, forks, pins, skewers, and beyond.

03

Game Analysis

Every game you play is reviewed with your coach. We identify decision patterns, time trouble habits, and the exact moments where improvement happens.

04

Compete Regularly

We integrate tournament play from the very beginning. Real competition under real pressure is irreplaceable — and we prepare you for it systematically.
